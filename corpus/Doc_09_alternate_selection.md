# Doc_09 — Alternate Aerodrome Selection Criteria

Source: Meridian Air Flight Operations Manual, Part A — Dispatch Planning (fictional carrier, synthetic data for demonstration purposes only)

## §1 Destination Alternate Requirement

A destination alternate is required unless the destination aerodrome forecast, for a period from 1 hour before to 1 hour after the estimated time of arrival, indicates a ceiling at least 2,000 feet above the lowest applicable minima and visibility at least 5 km, and two separate, independent runways are usable.

## §2 Alternate Weather Minima for Selection

An aerodrome may only be filed as an alternate if forecast weather at the estimated time of use meets: for a precision-approach-equipped alternate, ceiling at or above the CAT I DH plus 200 feet and visibility at or above the CAT I minimum plus 800 meters (a common "plus alternate minima" rule); for a non-precision-only alternate, ceiling and visibility at or above MDA plus 400 feet / 1.5 sm respectively.

## §3 En Route Alternate for International/EDTO Sectors

For EDTO sectors under Doc_03 §3, an en route alternate must be identified for every EDTO segment and must meet the EDTO-specific weather and performance minima at the expected time of use, independent of the destination alternate.

## §4 Number of Alternates Required

Two destination alternates are required if the destination itself does not meet the standalone no-alternate criteria in §1 and only one runway/approach combination is usable, or where destination weather is forecast to be at or near minima.
