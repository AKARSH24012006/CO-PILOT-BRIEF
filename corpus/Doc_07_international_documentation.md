# Doc_07 — International Flight Documentation Requirements

Source: Meridian Air Flight Operations Manual, Part E — Regulatory Documentation (fictional carrier, synthetic data for demonstration purposes only)

## §1 Documents Required Onboard — International Sectors

For any sector crossing an international boundary, the aircraft must carry: a valid Certificate of Airworthiness, Certificate of Registration, noise certificate, radio station license, general declaration (GenDec), passenger manifest, and insurance certificate, in addition to all documents required for domestic flights.

## §2 Crew Documentation

Flight and cabin crew operating international sectors must carry a valid passport with at least 6 months validity remaining, applicable visas or crew-member certificates for the destination and any diversion country, and a valid medical certificate.

## §3 Customs and Immigration Coordination

Dispatch must notify the destination station and any planned technical-stop or diversion-country handling agent at least 2 hours before departure for international sectors so that customs and immigration pre-clearance can be arranged.

## §4 Overflight Permits

Overflight permits must be confirmed valid for every country in the filed route before release. A route change after release (including a tactical reroute for weather) that adds a new overflight country without a valid permit requires dispatch to coordinate an alternate routing or delay.

## §5 Re-Designation of Domestic Flights to International

If operational circumstances (weather diversion, technical stop, alternate selection) cause a flight originally planned as domestic to route through or land in another country, the crew and dispatch must treat the flight as international from that point forward for documentation and customs notification. Fuel reserve treatment for such a re-designation is governed separately (see Doc_03 §3–§4), not by this section.
