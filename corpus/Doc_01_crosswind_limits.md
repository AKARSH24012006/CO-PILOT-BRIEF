# Doc_01 — Crosswind Limitations

Source: Meridian Air Flight Operations Manual, Part B — Performance Limitations (fictional carrier, synthetic data for demonstration purposes only)

## §1 Purpose and Scope

This section defines maximum demonstrated crosswind components for takeoff and landing across the Meridian Air fleet. Crosswind component is calculated as the wind speed multiplied by the sine of the angle between the wind direction and the runway heading. Values below are demonstrated limits, not certified structural limits, and are reduced further by runway contamination per Doc_08.

## §2 Fleet Crosswind Limits (Dry Runway)

- A320-family: 33 knots demonstrated crosswind, dry runway, both engines operating.
- B737-800/MAX: 30 knots demonstrated crosswind, dry runway.
- ATR 72-600: 25 knots demonstrated crosswind, dry runway.
- E190-E2: 28 knots demonstrated crosswind, dry runway.

## §3 Reduced Limits — Wet or Contaminated Runway

On a wet runway, all fleet crosswind limits in §2 are reduced by 5 knots. On a runway reported as "contaminated" (standing water, slush, snow, or ice per Doc_08 braking-action categories), crosswind limits are reduced by 10 knots from the dry-runway value, and operations are prohibited entirely if braking action is reported as "poor" or "nil".

## §4 Gusting Wind Adjustment

When gusts are reported, dispatch and the flight crew must use the peak gust value, not the mean wind speed, when computing the crosswind component for go/no-go decisions. A gust spread greater than 15 knots over the mean requires a dispatcher advisory note on the release.

## §5 Crew Discretion and Company Minimums

Commanders may impose a personal crosswind limit more conservative than the values in §2–§3 at their discretion; they may not exceed the published limits under any circumstance without a documented non-normal waiver from the Director of Flight Operations.
