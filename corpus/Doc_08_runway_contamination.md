# Doc_08 — Runway Contamination and Braking Action Reports

Source: Meridian Air Winter Operations Manual (fictional carrier, synthetic data for demonstration purposes only)

## §1 Braking Action Categories

Braking action is reported in five company-recognized categories: Good, Good-to-Medium, Medium, Medium-to-Poor, and Poor, plus "Nil" when no reliable braking is available. Reports may come from a Runway Condition Assessment (RCA) matrix code or a pilot braking-action report (PIREP).

## §2 Runway Condition Codes (RWYCC)

Runway Condition Codes range from 6 (dry) to 0 (nil braking). A RWYCC of 3 or below requires dispatch to recompute landing distance using contaminated-runway performance data before release or in-flight replanning.

## §3 Operational Restrictions by Braking Action

"Poor" braking action requires a dispatcher and commander joint risk assessment before departure or continued approach is authorized. "Nil" braking action prohibits takeoff and landing entirely until conditions improve and a new report is issued.

## §4 Interaction with Crosswind Limits

Braking action category directly reduces the maximum authorized crosswind component per Doc_01 §3. A "Medium-to-Poor" or worse report combined with any crosswind above 15 knots requires commander discretion review regardless of the published fleet limit.
