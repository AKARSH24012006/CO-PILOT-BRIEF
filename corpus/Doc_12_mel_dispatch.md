# Doc_12 — Minimum Equipment List (MEL) Dispatch Rules

Source: Meridian Air Maintenance Operations Manual (fictional carrier, synthetic data for demonstration purposes only)

## §1 MEL Categories and Rectification Intervals

MEL items are classified A (rectify per specified time, e.g., 10 days), B (3 days), C (10 days), or D (120 days), reflecting decreasing operational urgency. Category A items without a specified interval must be fixed before the next flight.

## §2 Dispatch Under MEL

An aircraft may be dispatched with an open MEL item only if the associated operational and maintenance procedures in the MEL are complied with and placarded, and the item is not required by the specific flight's operating rules (e.g., an anti-icing MEL item cannot be carried into forecast icing conditions).

## §3 Performance Penalties

Some MEL items impose a fuel burn or performance penalty (e.g., an inoperative thrust reverser, a fixed landing gear door). Dispatch must apply the published penalty to trip fuel and takeoff/landing performance calculations per Doc_03 §5 discretionary fuel guidance.

## §4 Multiple MEL Interaction

Two or more open MEL items affecting the same system are not automatically compatible; dispatch must consult the MEL's "or" conditions and interaction notes, and escalate to Maintenance Control if interaction guidance is not explicit.
