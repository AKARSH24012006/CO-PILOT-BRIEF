# Doc_15 — Emergency and Diversion Fuel Declaration Procedures

Source: Meridian Air Flight Operations Manual, Part D — Emergency Procedures (fictional carrier, synthetic data for demonstration purposes only)

## §1 "MINIMUM FUEL" Declaration

A crew declares "MINIMUM FUEL" to ATC when, having committed to land at a specific aerodrome, any change to the existing clearance may result in landing with less than planned Final Reserve Fuel (Doc_03 §1). This is an advisory, not a distress call, but requests priority handling awareness from ATC.

## §2 "MAYDAY FUEL" Declaration

A crew declares "MAYDAY FUEL" (a distress condition) when calculated usable fuel at landing at the nearest suitable aerodrome will be less than Final Reserve Fuel. This requires immediate priority handling and triggers company emergency response notification.

## §3 Post-Event Reporting

Any MINIMUM FUEL or MAYDAY FUEL declaration requires a mandatory occurrence report filed within 24 hours and triggers a dispatch/fuel-planning review by the Safety department.

## §4 Diversion Decision Authority

The Pilot-in-Command retains final authority to divert at any time based on fuel state, weather, or aircraft condition, regardless of dispatch's routing preference, and must notify dispatch as soon as practicable after the decision.
