# Doc_10 — Dispatch Release and Flight Plan Requirements

Source: Meridian Air Flight Operations Manual, Part A — Dispatch Authority (fictional carrier, synthetic data for demonstration purposes only)

## §1 Joint Authority

The Pilot-in-Command and the dispatcher share joint responsibility for the safety and legality of a flight release under the shared-authority dispatch system. Either may refuse or delay a release; the more conservative decision prevails.

## §2 Mandatory Release Contents

Every release must contain: route and altitude, fuel breakdown per Doc_03 §1, weight and balance summary, applicable NOTAMs per Doc_06, weather for departure/destination/alternate(s), and any MEL items per Doc_12 affecting the flight.

## §3 Release Validity and Amendment

A release becomes invalid if weather, NOTAMs, or MEL status materially change before departure, or if the flight's routing classification changes (e.g., domestic to international, per Doc_07 §5). An amended release must be issued and re-accepted by the commander before departure in these cases.

## §4 Reissue Triggers

Reissue is mandatory (not discretionary) for: alternate weather falling below §Doc_09 minima after release, a new MEL item restricting dispatch, fuel category changes per Doc_03 §4, or an EDTO alternate becoming unavailable.
