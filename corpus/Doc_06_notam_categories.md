# Doc_06 — NOTAM Categories and Interpretation

Source: Meridian Air Dispatch Reference Guide (fictional carrier, synthetic data for demonstration purposes only)

## §1 NOTAM Classes

NOTAMs relevant to dispatch fall into four company-tracked classes: Aerodrome (runway/taxiway closures, lighting outages), Airspace (temporary restricted areas, military activity), Navigation (navaid outages, GPS RAIM predictions), and Obstacle (new cranes, towers near approach/departure paths).

## §2 Dispatcher Review Requirement

Dispatch must review all NOTAMs valid for the departure, destination, alternate(s), and route of flight before release, and must specifically flag any NOTAM affecting the planned approach procedure, required navaid, or minimum safe altitude.

## §3 Runway/Taxiway Closure NOTAMs

A runway closure NOTAM removes that runway from consideration for takeoff/landing performance planning entirely; a partial closure (displaced threshold) requires recomputation of takeoff and landing distance using the reduced runway length.

## §4 NOTAM Validity and Briefing

Crews must be briefed on all class Aerodrome and Navigation NOTAMs for their route before pushback. Airspace and Obstacle NOTAMs are briefed when they affect the filed route or terminal procedures.
