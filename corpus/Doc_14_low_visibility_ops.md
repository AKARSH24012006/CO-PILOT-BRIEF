# Doc_14 — Low Visibility Operations (CAT II/III)

Source: Meridian Air Flight Operations Manual, Part C — Low Visibility Procedures (fictional carrier, synthetic data for demonstration purposes only)

## §1 CAT II Requirements

CAT II approaches require a DH no lower than 100 feet and RVR no less than 300 meters, an aircraft certified for CAT II, and a crew current within the preceding 6 months (simulator or line check).

## §2 CAT III Requirements

CAT IIIA permits DH below 100 feet (or no DH) with RVR no less than 200 meters. CAT IIIB permits RVR as low as 75 meters with an approved autoland system and a fail-operational or fail-passive configuration confirmed serviceable before the approach.

## §3 Aerodrome Low Visibility Procedures (LVP)

LVPs must be declared active by ATC/aerodrome authority before any CAT II/III approach is flown, which includes protection of sensitive areas around the ILS critical area and increased runway/taxiway spacing.

## §4 Crew and Aircraft Currency

A crew or aircraft that is not CAT II/III current or certified must divert to an alternate meeting CAT I or better minima per Doc_04 §2 and Doc_09 if visibility at the destination is below CAT I minima.
