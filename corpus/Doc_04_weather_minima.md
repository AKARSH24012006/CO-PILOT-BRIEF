# Doc_04 — Takeoff and Landing Weather Minima

Source: Meridian Air Flight Operations Manual, Part C — Aerodrome Operating Minima (fictional carrier, synthetic data for demonstration purposes only)

## §1 Takeoff Minima

Standard takeoff minima require a Runway Visual Range (RVR) of at least 400 meters for two-engine aircraft with functioning runway lighting, or 500 meters without centerline lighting. Below these values, takeoff is not authorized regardless of crew qualification.

## §2 Landing Minima — Category I (CAT I) ILS

CAT I approaches require a decision height (DH) of no lower than 200 feet and RVR/visibility no less than 550 meters (or 1/2 statute mile), unless the aerodrome chart specifies a higher company minimum.

## §3 Landing Minima — Category II and III (CAT II/III)

CAT II and III operations require specific aircraft certification, crew currency, and aerodrome infrastructure. See Doc_14 for full Low Visibility Operations procedures, required RVR values by category, and crew recency requirements.

## §5 Circling Approach Minima

Circling minima are never lower than straight-in minima for the same runway and are increased by aircraft approach category (A–D) per the published chart. Circling below 1,000 feet AGL ceiling is prohibited company-wide.

## §6 Non-Precision Approach Minima

For non-precision approaches without vertical guidance (LNAV-only, VOR, NDB), minimum descent altitude (MDA) plus a required visibility of at least 1 statute mile (or RVR 1600m) applies unless a steeper company-specific minimum is charted.
