# Doc_11 — Flight and Duty Time Limitations (FDTL)

Source: Meridian Air Crew Scheduling Manual (fictional carrier, synthetic data for demonstration purposes only)

## §1 Maximum Flight Duty Period (FDP)

Maximum unaugmented FDP for a two-pilot crew starting duty between 0600 and 1259 local is 13 hours for up to 2 sectors, reducing by 30 minutes per additional sector up to a floor of 10 hours for 6+ sectors.

## §2 Augmented Crew Extensions

With an augmented crew (3 or 4 pilots) and an approved rest facility, maximum FDP extends to 15 hours (3-pilot crew, Class 1 rest facility) or 17 hours (4-pilot crew, Class 1 rest facility), subject to in-flight rest period minimums.

## §3 Rest Period Requirements

Minimum rest before a duty period is the greater of 12 hours or the length of the preceding duty period, and must include a minimum 10-hour sleep opportunity at suitable accommodation for international layovers.

## §4 Cumulative Duty Limits

Cumulative duty may not exceed 60 hours in any 7 consecutive days, 110 hours in any 14 consecutive days, or 190 hours in any 28 consecutive days.
