# Doc_05 — Holding Procedures and Fuel Planning

Source: Meridian Air Flight Operations Manual, Part D — En Route and Terminal Procedures (fictional carrier, synthetic data for demonstration purposes only)

## §1 Standard Holding Pattern Dimensions

Standard holding patterns use 1-minute inbound legs below 14,000 feet and 1.5-minute inbound legs above 14,000 feet, with standard rate turns (3 degrees per second) unless a non-standard pattern is published.

## §2 Holding Fuel Planning

Dispatch must plan for expected holding at the destination based on historical congestion data and current ATC flow advisories. Planned holding fuel is added on top of Contingency Fuel (Doc_03 §1) and must be explicitly itemized on the release when expected delay exceeds 10 minutes.

## §3 Maximum Holding Before Diversion Decision

Crews should begin evaluating diversion to the filed alternate once holding fuel plus Final Reserve Fuel (Doc_03 §1) is reached, and must commit to diversion no later than the point where continuing to hold would leave less than Final Reserve Fuel available at the alternate.

## §4 Holding Speed by Altitude

Maximum holding speeds are 230 knots IAS at or below 6,000 feet, 240 knots IAS between 6,001 and 14,000 feet, and 265 knots IAS above 14,000 feet, unless ATC assigns a different speed.
